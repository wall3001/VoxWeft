VoxWeft is an on-device simultaneous interpretation system for Apple Silicon.
You speak into the microphone and it segments your speech while you are still talking; recognition, translation and synthesis all run on this machine, offline, and nothing leaves it.
At the core is our port of VoxCPM2 to MLX: a tokenizer-free speech model covering thirty languages that can clone a voice from five seconds of reference audio.
A streaming decoder gets the first sound out in under three hundred milliseconds.
Recognition and the language model are Lego bricks: we ship two defaults, and you can swap in your own.
We do not compete on BLEU. We make sure the pipeline loses nothing, and we wrote every mistake we made into the devlog.
